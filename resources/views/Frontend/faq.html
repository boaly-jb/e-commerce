Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, payments and returns. Still stuck? Contact us.

Orders

Browse the shop, add items to your cart, and check out with your address and preferred payment method. You'll get an order confirmation via email and SMS.

Yes — you can add or remove items within one hour of placing the order by contacting our support team.

We offer wholesale pricing on orders above 20kg for most pantry items. Reach out to hello@ghorebazar.com.
Delivery

We currently deliver across Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and Rajshahi, with next-day coverage in most metropolitan areas.

Delivery is a flat ৳80 for standard orders, and free on orders over ৳1,500.

Standard delivery is next-day if you order before 6 PM. Same-day delivery is available in select Dhaka zones.
Payments

Cash on delivery, card (Visa/Mastercard), and mobile banking (bKash, Nagad).

All card transactions are processed via a PCI-compliant gateway. We never store card details on our servers.
Returns

We accept returns on packaged goods within 7 days of delivery if unopened. Fresh produce can be returned or refunded on the same day if damaged.

Message us via the Contact page or WhatsApp and our team will schedule a pickup within 24 hours.
