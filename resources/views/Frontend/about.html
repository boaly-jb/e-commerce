Our story

A market, thoughtfully assembled.

Ghorebazar began as a weekly delivery run between a few farmer friends and a handful of families. Six years on, we are still building the same market — just for a larger table.

The Beginning

Groceries that feel personal.

We started in 2020 with a promise: every item on our shelves would be one we'd keep in our own pantries. That meant walking farms in Meherpur, tasting rice batches side-by-side, and choosing suppliers by handshake before contract.

Today Ghorebazar delivers to 40,000+ households across the country — still keeping the same rule.

Our mission

To build the most trusted everyday market — one where quality is a habit, not a promise. We source small, price fairly, and deliver reliably.

Our vision

A country where every family can shop from local farms and makers with the same convenience as any supermarket — and know exactly where their food comes from.
